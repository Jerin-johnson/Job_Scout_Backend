## Fantastic.jobs — storage, display, and change semantics questionnaire

**Subject:** Questions about API listing rights and update semantics for JobScout

Hello Fantastic.jobs team,

We are evaluating a small, capped integration for JobScout, a job-discovery product. Before any pilot, could you confirm in writing:

1. May we store and display returned listings, including full descriptions, in a commercial user-facing product? Are there separate rights for titles/snippets, descriptions, derived fields, embeddings, and paid plans?
2. Can the API be restricted to employer ATS-native postings only, excluding copies from aggregators or professional/social networks? Please describe the exact filter and provide source composition.
3. Can each returned row include its original employer/source URL, ATS/provider, original source job ID, and provenance suitable for display and deduplication?
4. What are the semantics for new, changed, repeated, closed, expired, and deleted records? Does an unchanged record consume the same quota as a new or changed record? How is a correction distinguished from a re-post?
5. What retention, caching, backup, export, and purge windows are permitted? How quickly must removals propagate, including from backups and derived data, after a listing closes or our contract ends?
6. What attribution text/link is required on each listing and in the product UI?
7. Could you share representative coverage samples for India (including Kerala), Gulf countries, nursing/healthcare, UK and Ireland, including the number of currently active listings and source types?
8. What request limits, retry guidance, webhook/change-feed options, and hard quota controls are available?

We will not cache or show listings until storage/display rights and source provenance are clear. Thank you,

[Name / organization / contact]
